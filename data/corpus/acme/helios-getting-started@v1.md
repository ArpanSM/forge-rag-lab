# Getting started with Helios

> Synthetic Helios documentation for the `acme` tenant. Version `v1`. Not real product documentation.

<!-- page: 1 -->

## Overview

<a id="overview"></a>

Helios is a project-management workspace for planning work, tracking tasks and sharing progress with your team. A workspace contains projects, members, saved views and billing settings.

## Create a workspace

<a id="create-a-workspace"></a>

Sign in at app.helios.example and choose Create workspace. Enter a workspace name, choose the US or EU data region, and invite at least one other owner. The region cannot be changed after the workspace is created.

## Invite your team

<a id="invite-your-team"></a>

Open Settings > Members and choose Invite people. Enter one email address per line, pick the Member role and send the invitations. Members can create projects and edit tasks; Viewers can only read.

<!-- page: 2 -->

## Create your first project

<a id="create-your-first-project"></a>

Open Projects and choose New project. Give the project a name, choose List or Timeline, and add the first tasks. Every project has a key such as OPS that prefixes task references like OPS-14.

## Notifications

<a id="notifications"></a>

By default Helios sends an email when someone mentions you, assigns you a task, or comments on a task you follow. Change the frequency under Profile > Notifications. A daily digest is sent at 08:00 in your workspace time zone.

## Next steps

<a id="next-steps"></a>

Import an existing backlog from CSV under Projects > Import. The importer accepts title, description, assignee, status and due date. You can also connect Slack from Settings > Integrations so task updates appear in a channel.
