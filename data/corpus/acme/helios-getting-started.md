# Getting started with Helios

> Synthetic Helios documentation for the `acme` tenant. Version `v3`. Not real product documentation.

<!-- page: 1 -->

## Overview

<a id="overview"></a>

Helios 3.0 is a project-management workspace with projects, tasks, milestones and workload planning. Press Command-K or Ctrl-K anywhere to open the command palette and jump to a project, task or setting.

## Create a workspace

<a id="create-a-workspace"></a>

Sign in at app.helios.example and choose Create workspace. Enter a workspace name, choose the US or EU data region, and invite at least one other owner. The region cannot be changed after the workspace is created.

## Invite your team

<a id="invite-your-team"></a>

Open Settings > Members and choose Invite people. Choose Member, Viewer or Guest. Guests only see the projects they are added to, while Viewers can read every project but cannot edit tasks or settings.

<!-- page: 2 -->

## Create your first project

<a id="create-your-first-project"></a>

Create a project from Projects > New project or from a workspace template. Choose List, Board, Timeline or Workload. Add tasks with an owner, due date, estimate, labels and dependencies.

## Use the command palette

<a id="use-the-command-palette"></a>

Press Command-K on macOS or Ctrl-K on Windows and Linux. Type a project name, task reference or setting. The palette also runs actions such as Create task, Assign to me and Toggle sidebar.

<!-- page: 3 -->

## Plan with milestones

<a id="plan-with-milestones"></a>

Milestones group tasks around a delivery date. Open a project, choose Milestones and add a target date. The timeline shows the milestone marker and warns when incomplete tasks fall after it.

## Next steps

<a id="next-steps"></a>

Invite your team, connect an integration and review the workspace audit log. Owners can enable SAML SSO and SCIM provisioning from Settings > Security.
