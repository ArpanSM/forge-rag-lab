# Acme procurement security addendum

> Synthetic Helios documentation for the `acme` tenant. Version `v3`. Not real product documentation.

<!-- page: 1 -->

## Confidentiality

<a id="confidentiality"></a>

Confidential — Acme only. This addendum applies to Acme's Helios workspace and must not be shared with another tenant. acme-private marker: ACME-PROC-4.

## Vendor review

<a id="vendor-review"></a>

Acme reviews Helios annually against its supplier security standard. The review covers access control, encryption, incident response and subprocessors. Acme data must remain in the EU region and may not be processed in the US region without written approval.

<!-- page: 2 -->

## Procurement approvals

<a id="procurement-approvals"></a>

New integrations that access employee data require approval from Acme procurement and the security team. Integrations that only post notifications may be installed by an Acme workspace Admin. All purchase orders must reference the current Helios order form.

## Contract termination

<a id="contract-termination"></a>

At contract termination Acme receives a full export within ten business days. Acme-specific retention commitment: Helios deletes the workspace 45 days after the termination date, after the export is confirmed. Backups containing Acme data expire within 35 days after deletion.
