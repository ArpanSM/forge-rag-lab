# Billing plans and invoices

> Synthetic Helios documentation for the `northwind` tenant. Version `v1`. Not real product documentation.

<!-- page: 1 -->

## Overview

<a id="overview"></a>

Helios bills per active member. Starter is free for up to five members, Team is USD 12 per member per month billed annually, and Business is USD 24 per member per month billed annually. Monthly billing costs 20 percent more.

<a id="overview-2"></a>

Starter includes three projects and 1 GB of storage. Team includes unlimited projects, 100 GB and standard support. Business includes 500 GB, SAML SSO and a 99.9 percent uptime target.

<!-- page: 2 -->

## Seats and billing cycle

<a id="seats-and-billing-cycle"></a>

A seat is an active member or guest. Viewers consume a seat. The billing cycle starts on the day the workspace is created and renews on the same day each month or year. Adding a seat mid-cycle is prorated; removing a seat takes effect at the next renewal.

## Invoices and payment

<a id="invoices-and-payment"></a>

Owners can download invoices from Settings > Billing. Card payments are charged automatically. Annual Business plans can request NET 30 terms during the sales process. Failed payments retry after three and seven days.

<!-- page: 3 -->

## Cancellation and refunds

<a id="cancellation-and-refunds"></a>

Cancelling ends the plan at the end of the current term. The workspace becomes read-only for 30 days and is then deleted. Monthly plans are not refunded. Annual plans may receive a refund for unused full months if cancelled within 14 days of purchase.

<a id="cancellation-and-refunds-2"></a>

Prices exclude taxes. Helios adds VAT, GST or sales tax when the billing address requires it.
